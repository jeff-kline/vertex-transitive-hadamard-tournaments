## The unresolved book locator and its mathematical application

In Lemma 33 the automorphism induced by $u$ on $E$ has order $p$ and $p$ is coprime to $|E|$. Write $\alpha(x)=uxu^{-1}$ and $A=\langle x\alpha(x)^{-1}:x\in E\rangle$. This subgroup is normal in $E$ since $y(x\alpha(x)^{-1})y^{-1}=(yx)\alpha(yx)^{-1}(y\alpha(y)^{-1})^{-1}$. It is invariant under $\alpha$, and $\alpha$ acts trivially on $E/A$. Each coset has size $|A|$ prime to $p$, so the action of $\langle\alpha\rangle$ on that coset fixes a representative. Thus every $x$ has form $ac$ with $a\in A$ and $\alpha(c)=c$. Then $x\alpha(x)^{-1}=a\alpha(a)^{-1}$. These generators show $[A,u]=A$. This checks the exact step needed in A and C regardless of the book's numbering.

For the standard background also cited collectively to Gorenstein, the text of B. Huppert, *Character Theory of Finite Groups*, preliminaries (5)–(10), pp. 7–8, states the Sylow, Hall, complement/conjugacy and Fitting-centralizer properties used in the draft. Complement conjugacy by the ambient group gives conjugacy by the normal factor because the complement normalizes itself. A minimal normal subgroup of a finite solvable group is elementary abelian by taking its last nonzero derived subgroup, then its prime torsion subgroup. The independent sources do not verify Gorenstein’s numbered locator. [Book text](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110809237_A19982281/preview-9783110809237_A19982281.pdf)
